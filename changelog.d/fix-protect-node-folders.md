### Security
- The usual folders where the zn hooks and plugins look for `node` and `zn` are now protected from writes by the agent: `~/.local/bin`, `~/bin`, `/usr/local/bin` (when you own it), the Node.js version manager folders (`~/.nvm`, `~/.volta`, `~/.fnm`, `~/.asdf/shims`) and their Windows equivalents. An agent could replace a `node` or `zn` there, and the hooks would then run it. File tools and shell commands can no longer write, replace, move, delete or link a `node` or `zn` in those folders, or delete or move the folders and their parents (such as `~/.local`). Reading and running what is there, creating the bin folders and installing your own tools in them, and project files such as `bin/node` still work. Copying into a bin folder is refused when a source could be a `node` or `zn` (a pattern such as `*` or `node*`, a folder's content, or files given by `xargs` or `find -exec`). Other places the `PATH` may lead to (mise, `PNPM_HOME`, `~/.npm-global/bin`) are not covered.
