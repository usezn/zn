### Security
- `zn team sync` refuses a second signed policy that carries the same version as the one already held but different content, and checks the version again when it saves the policy, so a policy replaced during a download is not overwritten. The organization's policy service signs the same version again every day with new validity dates; those dates are not part of the content, so the daily renewal is accepted and extends the validity, while an older signature of the same version cannot shorten it.
- `zn team join` no longer uploads the audit records written before you joined. Only records from the join on are sent; `zn team join <token> --include-history` adds the earlier ones, asks for it in its own warning in the confirmation dialog, and works only at the first join of an installation. A machine that joined with an earlier version keeps sending everything, as agreed then.

### Fixed
- The Team audit upload moves forward only as far as the server confirmed. A server answer that stored fewer records than were sent no longer marks the whole batch as uploaded, and an answer that contradicts the batch is reported as a failed upload.
- `zn team join` exits `1` unless the organization's first valid policy was downloaded and applied. The machine stays registered, `zn team status` shows the join as pending, and `zn team sync` finishes it.
- `zn team sync` exits `1` when it is offline, the organization has no policy, the policy is refused or an upload fails, instead of `0`. `zn team status` shows the reason of the last sync, and the automatic sync leaves a local audit record when it keeps failing (at most one every 6 hours).
- `zn team leave` checks that every local copy of the policy and the upload state (and the upload lock) was deleted. When one cannot be deleted it exits `1` and names the file. It also says that the server is not told: the installation stays on the organization's list until an admin removes it.
