### Added
- The local rules now catch the plain "ignore your previous instructions" order in Spanish, French, German and Portuguese ("ignora las instrucciones anteriores", "ignorez toutes les instructions", "vergiss deine bisherigen Regeln", "esqueça as instruções anteriores"). In these languages the verb counts when it names its target (instructions, rules, system prompt). A few fixed phrases also match with no target, such as "olvida todo", "oublie tout" or "vergiss alles", so ordinary sentences such as "ignora el error de compilación" or "vergiss den Termin nicht" stay allowed.
