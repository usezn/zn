### Fixed
- `zn audit` no longer reports text that discusses `curl | sh` as a risk (a checklist of risky patterns, a warning table, a rule that lists what to flag) as an instruction to download and run code. An install instruction is still reported when it sits under a heading such as "Don't forget" or "Vulnerability scanner", inside a code block, after words such as "detect" or "look for", after "without verification", behind a "Critical:" label or in a table row with an errors column or a "no risk", "not blocked", "risk-free" or "isn't risky" note, after a warning followed by "but", "so", a colon, a dash or an arrow ("the old one was unsafe: curl ... | sh", "unsafe -> curl ... | sh"), or after a label such as "Avoid:" with an order later in the line. Audit rules version 2026-10-23.7.
