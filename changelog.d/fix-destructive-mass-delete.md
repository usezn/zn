### Security
- The destructive-command check (`rm_root_home`) now also blocks mass deletes it let through on the home folder, the filesystem root or a top-level system folder: `find` with `-delete`, `-exec rm`, `-execdir rm` or `-ok rm` even when it has a test such as `-name` or `-type`, `find` on those folders piped to `xargs rm` (also with `-print0` and `xargs -0`), and `git clean -f` with `-C`, `--work-tree` or a path naming one of them. `find ... -print`, `git clean -n` and `git clean -i`, and the same commands on a project or another folder below the top level, are still allowed.
