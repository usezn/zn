### Security
- The MCP shield now also catches an injection whose halves are not next to each other. Harmless text fields can stand between them (up to four), also over several items of a tool result or inside structured data, and the halves are still read together. The content and the structured content of a tool result are checked as one text in every case, including an answer that does not match a request. The extra check only reads the first and last characters of each long field, so large results are not slowed down in proportion to their size. A split with more than four fields between the halves, or into three or more pieces at uneven distances, is not covered.
