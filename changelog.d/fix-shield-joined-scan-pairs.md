### Security
- The MCP shield now also catches an injection whose halves are not next to each other. Harmless text fields can stand between them (up to four), also over several items of a tool result or inside structured data, and the halves are still read together. The content and the structured content of a tool result are checked as one text in every case, including an answer that does not match a request. The extra check only reads the first and last characters of each long field, so it adds little to large results; a result made of many short fields can take up to about a third longer to check. Not covered: a split with more than four fields between the halves, a split into three or more pieces at uneven distances, halves placed in object keys, and a credential request split across fields that are not neighbours.
