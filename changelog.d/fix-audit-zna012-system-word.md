### Fixed
- `zn audit` no longer reports a prompt-injection finding for placeholders such as `<system-or-project>` or for values such as `system:anonymous` in the supporting notes of a skill. A placeholder tag that is followed by text, or that has a matching closing tag anywhere in the file, stays reported, and chat-template tokens shown in code there are reported as medium instead of high. Audit rules version 2026-10-23.3.
