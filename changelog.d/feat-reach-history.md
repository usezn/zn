### Added
- `zn-gate reach --history` shows whether your coding agents kept secrets of yours in their local session history. It reads the Claude Code and Codex history files in your home folder, line by line, and prints the agent, the file, the secret type, how many times and what to do (rotate the key, delete the session), never a value, and `--json` gives the same for scripts. It writes nothing, sends nothing and follows no link, and it says which files it did not finish when its time limit (`--max-seconds`, default 120) is reached. It cannot see what was already sent to your model provider.
