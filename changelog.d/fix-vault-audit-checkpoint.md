### Security
- The vault audit check (`zn vault verify`) now compares the last record the vault remembers by sequence number and MAC, not only by record count. The vault also compares the log with that record before it unlocks and before every write: if the log was cut or replaced while the vault was locked or running, the vault keeps the old checkpoint, stores a warning that survives lock and unlock, and `zn vault verify` fails until you accept the log with `zn vault verify --acknowledge` in your own terminal. Audit records are now flushed to disk when written. Limits: restoring an older `secrets.age`, with or without its older `audit.jsonl`, is not detected, and if the vault process is killed, records written since its last save (up to a minute) can be cut without notice.
