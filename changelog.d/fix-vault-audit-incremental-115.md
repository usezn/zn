### Changed
- Adding a record to the vault's audit log no longer checks every signature of the whole log, so a vault with a long log stays fast (about 570 ms per write at 100,000 records before, about 11 ms now). A write still compares the log with the last record the vault remembers and checks the records just before it, and the whole chain is still checked when the vault unlocks, before `zn vault log`, `zn vault log export --otlp` and `zn team sync` use the log, and before a write when the last full check is more than a minute old. A record changed in the middle of the log is therefore reported at the next of those checks instead of by the write itself; the log is still never shown, exported or uploaded while it does not verify.
