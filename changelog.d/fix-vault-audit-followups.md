### Security
- Hardening of the audit log check in the unlocked vault. A change made to the log in the instant between the vault checking it and adding its next record is no longer counted as checked, so the next read, export or upload checks the log again and reports it. `zn vault log` no longer falls back to the unchecked log when the vault is unlocked but cannot check it (for example an older vault process still running after an upgrade); it says so and asks you to restart the vault. The warning of `zn vault log export --otlp` now says "locked or unreachable" when it cannot reach the vault.
