### Changed
- When `ZN_HOOK_DIR` is refused, the message now names the folder that fails and why, says in plain words when a folder is missing or cannot be used, gives `chmod g-w,o-w <folder>` (no `sudo`) for a folder you own, and suggests a folder that works on your machine (not `/opt` where it is a link, as on Fedora Silverblue). A shared folder you own (mode 1777) now gets the shared-folder notice instead of a `chmod`, and the suggested `sudo mkdir` also gives the new folder to you.
