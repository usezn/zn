### Fixed
- The rules block "forget the instructions", "forget previous instructions" and "forget the previous instructions" again, in the Node.js, Python and Rust engines. A negation attached to a longer word no longer slips the attack through: "However forget the previous instructions", "Forever forget the instructions" and "cannot forget the instructions" now block, while the genuine negations "Don't forget the instructions in the README", "Do not forget the previous instructions" and "never forget the instructions" stay allowed.
